# A voice script builder with multi-person parts and summarising

## Scripts

- Creates a different colour, highlighting the lines for different people in a script
  - Seperated into sections or scenes if the section is small

## Recordings

- Can record voices. Each highlighted or assigned section (scene) can have a recordings
  - Status for those lines, (unrecorded, in progress, done)
  - Redo button on any done/in progress line
  - Storage? Google drive?
- Sharing with friends, linking to the webpage, highlighted sections.

- Some of the recordings have backing tracks, can we pair up lines to backing tracsks?
- Can have backing tracks as well as singing and stuff.

## User Flow

J as admin uploads a script.
J Highlights a section and assigns it to E (With a highlighter / colour?).
J can assign a backing track to the section from storage (optional)
E Clicks on the section, brings up a recording button. Allows them to record, re-record, delete. Over a backing track if assigned.
Recordings are saved on a drive?
J and E can both hear a summary of what has been recorded.
